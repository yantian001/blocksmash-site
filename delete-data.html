Block Smash – Data Deletion

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Application ID: com.lt.blocksmash · Developer: FPS Shooter & Action Game

This page explains how to delete the data associated with the mobile game Block Smash. The Game does not create an account of its own: it has no username, password or profile stored on our servers, and we keep no server-side personal data. The data that can exist is described in our Privacy Policy and can be deleted as follows.

1. Data stored on your device

Level progress, coins, lives, boosters, language and settings are stored locally. Uninstalling the Game deletes all of them.

2. Google Play Games data (if you signed in)

If you signed in with Google Play Games, your player ID, nickname and avatar link, achievements, leaderboard scores and cloud save for Block Smash are stored by Google in your Google account. You can delete them yourself:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile picture or Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under "Delete individual game data", pick Block Smash and confirm. This removes your achievements, leaderboard scores and cloud save for the Game. You may also delete your entire Play Games profile there.

You can also review and manage this data from your Google Account (Data & privacy). To stop future syncing without deleting anything, sign out of Play Games in Android Settings → Google → All services → Play Games.

3. Advertising ID

Ads in the Game are served by Google AdMob using your device's advertising ID. To reset or delete it, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. You can change your ad consent choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options.

4. Analytics and other partner data – request by email

Usage events collected by Firebase Analytics, ad interaction data held by AdMob and purchase records held by Unity IAP are linked to anonymous identifiers, not to your name. To ask us to delete the data our partners hold about your installation, email topactionsgame@gmail.com with the subject "Block Smash data deletion" and include:

  • the app name (Block Smash);
  • the approximate date you installed the Game and the device model you used;
  • your Play Games nickname, if you signed in with Play Games.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion with our partners within 30 days. We may ask for additional details only where needed to locate the data. In-app purchase records needed for refunds, accounting or legal obligations may be retained by Google Play for the period required by law.

5. Questions

Contact topactionsgame@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.

Block Smash 数据删除

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日 · 应用 ID：com.lt.blocksmash · 开发者：FPS Shooter & Action Game

本页说明如何删除与手机游戏 Block Smash 相关的数据。本游戏不创建自有账号：没有用户名、密码或保存在我们服务器上的资料，我们自身不在服务器端保存任何个人数据。可能存在的数据见隐私政策，可按以下方式删除。

一、保存在你设备上的数据

关卡进度、金币、生命、道具、语言和设置保存在本地。卸载游戏即全部删除。

二、Google Play 游戏数据（如你已登录）

如果你登录了 Google Play 游戏，你的玩家 ID、昵称与头像关联、成就、排行榜分数和 Block Smash 的云存档由 Google 保存在你的 Google 账号中。你可以自行删除：

  1. 在 Android 设备上打开 Google Play 游戏应用。
  2. 点击你的头像或设置。
  3. 选择删除 Play 游戏账号和数据。
  4. 在「删除单个游戏的数据」下选择 Block Smash 并确认。这会删除本游戏的成就、排行榜分数和云存档。你也可以在此删除整个 Play 游戏资料。

你也可以在 Google 账号（数据和隐私）中查看和管理这些数据。如只想停止同步而不删除，可在 Android 设置 → Google → 所有服务 → Play 游戏中退出登录。

三、广告 ID

游戏内广告由 Google AdMob 通过设备的广告 ID 投放。要重置或删除广告 ID，请进入 Android 设置 → Google → 广告（或 设置 → 隐私 → 广告），选择重置广告 ID 或删除广告 ID。你随时可以在游戏内「设置 → 隐私选项」更改广告同意选择。

四、统计分析及其他合作方数据——邮件申请

Firebase Analytics 收集的使用事件、AdMob 持有的广告互动数据、Unity IAP 持有的购买记录都关联匿名标识，而非你的姓名。如需我们删除合作方持有的与你安装相关的数据，请发邮件至 topactionsgame@gmail.com，主题写「Block Smash 数据删除」，并提供：

  • 应用名称（Block Smash）；
  • 大致安装时间和使用的设备型号；
  • 如你登录过 Play 游戏，提供 Play 游戏昵称。

我们会确认收到，并在 30 天内完成与合作方的删除。仅在定位数据确有必要时，我们才会向你索取补充信息。退款、记账或法律义务所需的应用内购买记录，可能由 Google Play 按法律要求的期限保留。

五、联系我们

邮箱：topactionsgame@gmail.com。另见隐私政策。
