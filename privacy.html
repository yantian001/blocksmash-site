Block Smash – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Application ID: com.lt.blocksmash · Developer: FPS Shooter & Action Game

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Block Smash (the "Game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information we collect

The Game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

1.1 Collected automatically by third-party services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) from the partners below. They may automatically collect the following information from your device when you use the Game:

PartnerPurposeInformation
Google AdMob (Google LLC)Showing rewarded and interstitial advertisementsAdvertising ID, IP address, device type and model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction data (views, clicks), and app identifiers
Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC)Understanding how the Game is used so we can fix problems and improve levelsApp instance ID, device model and OS version, app version, language, country derived from IP, and in-game events such as level started, level completed, booster used, ad watched, and purchase completed. Events do not contain your name or contact details.
Google Play Billing (Google LLC)Processing in-app purchasesPurchase token, product ID and order ID. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are handled entirely by Google Play and are never visible to us.

Each partner processes this information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy, AdMob & Google ads data, Firebase privacy.

1.2 Stored only on your device

Your level progress, coins, lives, booster inventory, language choice and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the Game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show advertisements, which keep the Game free. Where required by law, ads are personalised only with your consent (see section 4).
  • To measure how the Game is used, find crashes and balance level difficulty, in aggregated form.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

Information is shared only with the partners listed in section 1.1, for the purposes stated there, and where required by law or to protect our rights. These partners may transfer data to servers outside your country, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

4. Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): on first launch the Game shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from the in-game Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: the Game offers an optional "No Ads" purchase that disables interstitial and banner ads.
  • Analytics: analytics collection follows the same consent choice as advertising where consent is required.
  • Delete data: uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of data held by our partners, use the links in section 1.1 or contact us.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your region) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security and retention

We keep no server-side personal data of our own. Data collected by our partners is retained according to their policies (for example, Google Analytics for Firebase event data is retained for up to 14 months by default).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address, with the effective date shown at the top.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: topactionsgame@gmail.com

Block Smash 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日 · 应用 ID：com.lt.blocksmash · 开发者：FPS Shooter & Action Game

本隐私政策说明手机游戏 Block Smash（下称"本游戏"）收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及你可以做出的选择。安装或使用本游戏即表示你同意本政策；如不同意，请不要使用本游戏。

一、我们收集的信息

本游戏不要求注册账号，不收集你的姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、照片或精确位置。

1.1 由第三方服务自动收集

本游戏集成了以下合作方的 SDK，它们可能在你使用本游戏时自动从设备收集以下信息：

合作方用途信息
Google AdMob（Google LLC）展示激励视频和插页广告广告 ID、IP 地址、设备型号、系统版本、语言、由 IP 推算的大致位置、广告互动数据（展示、点击）、应用标识
Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase（Google LLC）了解游戏的使用情况，用于修复问题和调整关卡应用实例 ID、设备型号与系统版本、应用版本、语言、由 IP 推算的国家，以及游戏内事件（开始关卡、通关、使用道具、观看广告、完成购买等）。事件不包含你的姓名或联系方式。
Google Play 结算（Google LLC）处理应用内购买购买令牌、商品 ID、订单号。支付信息（银行卡号等）完全由 Google Play 处理，我们无法看到。

各合作方按其自身隐私政策处理上述信息：Google 隐私权政策、AdMob 与 Google 广告数据、Firebase 隐私。

1.2 仅保存在你的设备上

关卡进度、金币、生命、道具库存、语言选择和设置都保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器。卸载游戏即删除。

二、信息的用途

  • 运行游戏并在你的设备上保存进度。
  • 展示广告以维持游戏免费。在法律要求的地区，只有在你同意后才展示个性化广告（见第四条）。
  • 以汇总方式统计游戏使用情况、发现崩溃、平衡关卡难度。
  • 交付和恢复应用内购买。

我们不出售你的个人信息。

三、信息共享

信息仅为上述目的共享给第 1.1 节列出的合作方，或在法律要求、维护我们合法权益时披露。这些合作方可能把数据传输到你所在国家/地区之外的服务器（包括美国），并采用其自身的保护措施。

四、你的选择

  • 广告同意（欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士及美国部分州）：首次启动时游戏会显示 Google User Messaging Platform 提供的同意表单。你随时可以在游戏内「设置 → 隐私选项」更改选择。
  • 广告 ID：可在设备设置中重置或删除广告 ID、关闭广告个性化（Android：设置 → Google → 广告）。
  • 去广告：游戏提供可选的「去广告」购买项，关闭插页和横幅广告。
  • 统计分析：在需要同意的地区，分析数据的收集遵循与广告相同的同意选择。
  • 删除数据：卸载游戏即删除本地全部数据。如需删除合作方持有的数据，请使用第 1.1 节中的链接或联系我们。

五、儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下（或你所在地区数字同意年龄以下）的儿童，我们不会故意收集他们的个人信息。如你认为有儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会删除。

六、安全与保留

我们自身不在服务器端保存任何个人数据。合作方收集的数据按其政策保留（例如 Google Analytics for Firebase 的事件数据默认最长保留 14 个月）。

七、政策变更

本政策可能不时更新。最新版本始终发布在本地址，顶部标注生效日期。

八、联系我们

有关隐私的问题或请求：topactionsgame@gmail.com
