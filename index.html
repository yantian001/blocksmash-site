How to play

怎么玩

1

Tap a cannon

点一下炮台

A queue of colored cannons waits at the bottom. Tap one and it slides into one of five seats.

屏幕下方排着一队彩色炮台，点一下它就入座，座位只有五个。

2

Watch it fire

看它开火

A seated cannon locks onto blocks of its own color and keeps firing until its ammo runs out.

入座的炮台自动瞄准模型上同色的方块连续射击，子弹打完就离场。

3

Keep the seats open

别让炮位堵住

Seat a color with nothing left to hit and that seat stays stuck. Read the model and choose the right order.

模型上没这个颜色了，炮台就会卡住；看清模型、按对顺序上炮台才能打到底。
